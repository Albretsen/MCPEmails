mcpemails speaks plain IMAP and SMTP, so it connects almost any mailbox. Below is every provider with a setup guide: the exact hosts and ports, whether you need an app password, and what tends to go wrong.
mcpemails connects to any email provider that offers IMAP and SMTP access, which is nearly all of them. Each guide below lists that provider's verified hostnames, ports and authentication requirements, and one inbox is free forever.
Every hostname on these pages was checked on 2026-08-31 by opening a real TLS connection to it.
