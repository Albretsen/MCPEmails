What it actually does
Real mailboxes, not a read-only peek
The same tools work on every mailbox you connect. With several connected, each call names the mailbox it acts on.
Read and search
List, read and search each connected mailbox, fetched live from the provider every time.
Send, reply and forward
Send new mail, reply in the thread, or forward with the original attachments intact, from the address the mail belongs to.
Schedule
Queue a message to go out later, and cancel it any time before it is sent.
Organize
Move, copy, flag, archive and label messages, and create the folders to file them in.
Control
You stay the one who presses send
Letting an agent into the company's mail only works if the limits are real. These are enforced on our server, not suggested to the model.
Approval hold on outgoing mail
Turn it on per mailbox and every send, reply, forward, draft send and scheduled send waits in your dashboard until you approve or reject it. It is included on every plan, Free too.
The mail it reads is never stored
Messages are fetched live from your provider for each request and handed to your AI client, with nothing kept between calls. What we store per mailbox is the encrypted token or password. A message you schedule for later is the exception: we store it until it is sent, and keep a record in your history afterwards.
Keys limited to the mailboxes you pick
An API key can be restricted to selected mailboxes and to the permissions it needs. A key made for triaging support@ cannot reach invoices@, and a key without the send permission cannot send at all.
Your mail server stays in charge
Mail is sent through your own provider or SMTP server, never from a mail server of ours. Changing a mailbox password, revoking its app password, or removing access in your Google or Microsoft account cuts the agent off.

Any host
Connect every mailbox the company runs, whoever hosts it
Most company mail lives with the web host or the domain registrar, and that is fine. Any mailbox that speaks IMAP and SMTP on the standard ports connects with its address, host and password. IONOS, Hostinger, OVH, Namecheap and other common hosts are recognised from the address and filled in for you. Gmail and Google Workspace connect with an app password or by signing in with Google. Microsoft 365 and Outlook connect by signing in with Microsoft; on a company Microsoft 365 account, your IT admin may need to approve mcpemails once for the whole organisation. iCloud, Fastmail, Yahoo, Zoho and Yandex connect with an app password.

Operator questions
What operators ask before connecting
The practical objections, answered straight.
We are a company, not an individual. Is Pro really the right plan?
Yes, if one person operates the mailboxes. Pro is one login with no limit on connected mailboxes, so a single operator can run info@, sales@, invoices@ and support@ from one agent. Team is for when several people each need to sign in, with roles and a separate workspace per client.
Our email is with our web host, not Gmail. Will it work?
Almost certainly. Any mailbox that speaks IMAP and SMTP on the standard ports connects with its address, host and password, and common hosts are recognised from the address so the settings fill themselves in. The server has to be reachable from the public internet, and a mailbox that will only accept an OAuth sign-in over IMAP cannot be connected this way. If your company mail is on Microsoft 365, connect it as Outlook instead: you sign in with Microsoft, and your IT admin may need to approve mcpemails once for the whole organisation.
Does the agent send from the right address?
Yes. With more than one mailbox connected, every tool call names the mailbox it acts on, and each mailbox has its own sender name and signature. A reply to a thread in sales@ goes out from sales@, through that mailbox's own mail server.
Can I stop it sending anything without me?
Yes. Turn on the approval hold for a mailbox and every outgoing message from it waits in your dashboard until you approve or reject it. You can also give an AI client an API key that has no send permission at all. Both are available on every plan.
Is company email stored on your servers?
No. The mail your agent reads is fetched live from your provider for each request and is not kept. What we store is the encrypted token or password for each mailbox. A message you schedule for later is the exception: we store it until it is sent, and keep a record in your history afterwards.
What if a colleague needs access too?
Free, Personal and Pro are single-login plans. When a second person needs a login of their own, Team adds members with roles. Until then, one operator on Pro covers every mailbox.
