2. Description of Service
MCPEmails provides a software-as-a-service platform that allows you to connect email accounts (Gmail, Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365, Fastmail, and compatible IMAP accounts) and expose those accounts to AI agents via the Model Context Protocol (MCP). The Service includes:
A web dashboard for managing connected email accounts, API keys, and usage monitoring.
An MCP-compliant HTTP endpoint that AI agents authenticate against using API keys you generate.
OAuth integration with supported email providers to obtain and manage delegated access to your mailboxes.
A usage and audit log showing which tools your agents have called and when.
MCPEmails acts as a protocol bridge. We do not store the email content your agent reads on our servers; we retrieve it live from your email provider at the moment an agent requests it and return it in the same HTTP response. The exception is a message you schedule to send later, which we store only until it is delivered. See our
Privacy Policy
for a full description of what data we do and do not retain.
Email content is delivered only to the MCP client or AI agent that you have connected and choose to use. Once content reaches that client, it is governed by that client's or AI provider's own privacy policy and terms, which you are responsible for reviewing. MCPEmails does not select an AI provider on your behalf and retains no copy of your email content.
We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any part of the Service at any time. Where practicable we will give at least 30 days' notice of material changes to paid users.
